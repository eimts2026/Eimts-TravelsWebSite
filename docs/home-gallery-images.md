# Homepage travel gallery images

Six distinct user-supplied photographs were enhanced using the built-in imagegen tool, then exported as WebP images and separate 160 × 100 thumbnails in `public/images/home-gallery/`. These are AI-enhanced interpretations; fine details may differ from the original photographs.

The duplicate train attachment is represented once. The homepage’s existing three-photo moments section is replaced; the Tailor-made contact section remains.

## Enhancement prompt

Applied separately to the waterfall, Sigiriya, tea-country, palm-coast and train photographs:

> Enhance and upscale this exact travel photograph as a single standalone landscape photograph, ideally 2048 pixels wide. Preserve original aspect ratio, framing, geography, objects, lighting and colors. Improve natural detail, clarity, texture, compression artifacts and sharpness without oversharpening or inventing new objects. No collage, no text. Save the enhanced image for a website gallery.

Final prompt used for the fishermen photograph:

> Edit ONLY the attached photograph of Sri Lankan stilt fishermen standing on wooden poles in the sea at sunset. Upscale and enhance detail and clarity of this exact image. Preserve all fishermen, wooden poles, water reflections, pastel sunset sky, horizon and original 3:2 landscape composition. No rocks, no mountains, no forests, no other photograph, no collage. Output one high resolution photograph of the stilt fishermen.
